### Welcome to Anemone

Anemone is a literary and visual arts review published in Montana. We center short fiction, creative nonfiction, reportage, poetry, photography, and visual art that capture the world as told by Montanans.

> "To winnow that pile to finalists, our editorial team sought equal parts craft and conviction."

### In This Issue

- **Letter from the Editor**: *"Are you having any fun?"* — Reflections on independent publishing, salesmanship, and original voices.
- **Submission Guidelines**: Details on Vol. 1, No. 2 submission deadlines, category specifications, and honorariums ($200 per published piece).
- **Docx Blog Archive**: Field notes, raw drafts, and essay archives parsed dynamically.
