<article class="blog-article">
  <blockquote>
    “Are you having any fun?”
  </blockquote>

  <p>
    I began to ask myself this question in a prospective advertiser’s ski gear shop, a few months back.
    Of course, this whole thing—putting together an analog review in an age of frictionless digital consensus—requires asking what space physical media still occupies.
  </p>

  <p>
    This project began with a call for entries. Scores of writers from around Montana and beyond submitted work. Telling trends emerged: a deep, lingering desire for tactile literature, deliberate typography, and unhurried reportage.
  </p>

  <p>
    Anemone was born out of that shared impulse. Thank you for reading, supporting, and keeping the analog perspective alive.
  </p>
</article>
