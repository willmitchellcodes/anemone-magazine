<article class="blog-article">
  <p>
    Anemone welcomes submissions of short fiction, creative nonfiction, reportage, poetry, photography, and visual art.
  </p>

  <h3 style="margin-top: 1.5rem; margin-bottom: 0.5rem; color: var(--text-primary);">Editorial Focus &amp; Scope</h3>
  <p>
    Broadly: the world as told through an analog perspective, anchored in high-contrast narrative grit and reflective observation.
  </p>

  <blockquote style="border-left-color: var(--accent-crimson);">
    A few words to the wise: use AI to originate work and you will be choked out with a Fubuki.
  </blockquote>

  <h3 style="margin-top: 1.5rem; margin-bottom: 0.5rem; color: var(--text-primary);">Compensation &amp; Terms</h3>
  <p>
    Any and all accepted works will never be published without net-ninety (maximum) payment arrangements.
  </p>

  <h3 style="margin-top: 1.5rem; margin-bottom: 0.5rem; color: var(--text-primary);">How to Submit</h3>
  <p>
    Send electronic manuscript files (.docx or plain text) to <a href="mailto:staff@anemonereview.com" style="color: var(--accent-crimson); text-decoration: underline;">staff@anemonereview.com</a>.
  </p>
</article>
