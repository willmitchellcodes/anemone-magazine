Short fiction, creative nonfiction, reportage, poetry, photography, and visual art all welcome.

**As for theme:** broadly, the world as told by Montanans.

> **A few words to the wise:** use AI to originate work and you will be choked out with a swift rejection. No AI slop.

Specifications and guidelines for each category are noted below:

### Honorariums
**$200** per published long-form creative nonfiction, short fiction, and reportage.

---

### Creative Nonfiction & Essays
* No longer than 5,000 words per entry.
* All unpublished works welcome, written in any year, for any purpose (e.g. a class).
* As best as one reasonably can, relate the piece to themes, pitfalls, joys, etc. of the day.
* Submit document with file extension: `lastname_creativenonfiction_entrynumber` (e.g. `smith_creativenonfiction_1.docx`).
* Include full name (pen names OK) and contact email at the top of the document.

### Short Fiction
* No longer than 5,000 words per entry.
* Unpublished submissions only.
* All styles welcome.
* Submit document with file extension: `lastname_fiction_entrynumber` (e.g. `smith_fiction_1.docx`).

### Reportage & Literary Journalism
* Reports should center events/topics of evergreen value.
* Pieces should be executed in literary journalism form.
* Submit document with file extension: `lastname_originalreport_entrynumber`.

### Poetry
* No word count restrictions. But no epics, please!
* All structures welcome.
* Unpublished submissions only (exceptions made for self-published work with minimal online traction).
* Submit document with file extension: `lastname_poem_entrynumber`.

### Visual Art & Photography
* Resolutions between 150–300 dpi.
* Files must be in `.jpeg`, `.jpg`, or `.png` extensions.
* Submit a single written document describing/captioning the photo(s), including date, location, title, and contact info.
